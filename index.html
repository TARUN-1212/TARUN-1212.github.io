I'm Sai Tarun Baratam, a researcher focused on building hardware that is secure against both classical and quantum threats. My work lives at the crossroads of post-quantum cryptography and hardware implementation.

I design and attack cryptographic circuits on FPGAs and ASICs, with a particular interest in side-channel attacks — because a mathematically secure algorithm can still leak secrets through its silicon.
